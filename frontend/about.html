About ChangeGuard

Agentic, evidence-grounded risk assessment for enterprise IT changes.

The Problem

Every production change in a large IT organisation must clear a Change Advisory Board (CAB). This review is manual, slow, and inconsistent — low-risk changes are rubber-stamped while genuinely risky ones slip through, and poor change approval is a leading cause of major IT outages.

How ChangeGuard Works

Given a change request, ChangeGuard acts as an agent: it gathers evidence, reasons over it, and produces an auditable recommendation.

1. ML Risk Model — predicts failure probability from change attributes.
2. Similar-Change Retrieval (FAISS) — finds comparable past changes and their outcomes.
3. Evidence Tools — incident history, schedule conflicts, rollback status.
4. LLM Reasoning — weighs all evidence and writes a cited justification.

Why It Matters

Every recommendation is grounded in retrieved evidence, so decisions are consistent, faster, and auditable — assisting human CAB reviewers rather than replacing them.
